LSP-mdita 1.5.0
===============

Tracks mdita-lsp v1.3.0.

Requires mdita-lsp 1.3.0 or newer, and the aireilly/org.lwdita
plug-in fork, 6.4.0 or newer:

    https://github.com/aireilly/mdita-lsp/releases/latest
    https://github.com/aireilly/org.lwdita/releases/latest

What changed
------------

  In a concept or a reference, ## is a section. The plug-in builds it
  as <section> in <conbody> or <refbody>, which is the topic an author
  gets from an XML editor. The outline marks those headings as
  sections, and hover names the DITA element.

  Sections do not nest, so ### under one is an error with a quick fix
  that changes it to ##.

  A link to a section needs the topic id. file.md#heading draws a
  warning with a quick fix that rewrites it to
  file.md#topic-id/heading, and anchor completion offers the qualified
  form. A link to the topic itself, with or without its id, is
  unaffected.

  Generic topics and tasks are unchanged: ## there still opens a
  nested topic. The ordering trap, where a {.section} heading follows
  a heading that opened a nested topic, now names both headings and
  offers both ways out.

  The concept and reference snippets insert a ## section.
